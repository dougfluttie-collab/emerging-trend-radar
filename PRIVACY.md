# Privacy Policy — Emerging Trend Radar

Last updated: 2026-10-16

Emerging Trend Radar is an internal, non-public research tool operated by one independent researcher. It has no end users, no accounts, and no user-facing interface.

## Use of YouTube API Services
This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's Privacy Policy applies to data accessed through YouTube API Services: http://www.google.com/policies/privacy

## What information is accessed and stored
- Public video metadata (video IDs, titles, publish dates) and public statistics (view, like and comment counts) for videos matching searches for consumer brand names.
- No personal information about any individual is requested, collected or stored. No user data is accessed, and no Google or YouTube sign-in (OAuth) is used.

## How the information is used and shared
- Data is used only for internal research on aggregate attention to consumer brands.
- Data is not sold, shared, displayed to, or transferred to any third party.
- The tool serves no advertising and places no cookies or similar technology on any device.

## Retention and deletion
- Video titles and other non-statistical metadata are refreshed or deleted within 30 days.
- Public statistics are deleted or refreshed within 30 days, unless YouTube has accepted this client for the Additional Policies for Derived Metrics and Data Storage, in which case statistics and derived metrics are retained for no more than 36 months.
- Any user who believes data related to them is stored may request deletion at the contact below; it will be deleted within 7 days. Google account permissions can be reviewed at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions
